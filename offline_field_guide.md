## Problem Statement
I Often I have found myself in the middle of my property working on something, but I need a guide on how to construct something, but there is no cell reception and a youtube video takes way too long to load or it's humid out and I don't want to bring a paper guide. I have tried to make sure I downloaded the book or reference guide I wanted to my phone, only to head way out into the field and find out my stupid phone off loaded the file to save on space. So then I have to ride all the way back to an access point to  access the internet and download it again.

---
It would be nice if I could store some PDFs on the UTV manager, be able to search through and see pictures or have a properly trained AI to walk me through the steps or answer questions offline while I'm in the field.
